NEED A FEW ANSWERS?

Good to Know Before Delivery Day.

Find answers to common delivery and installation questions. If your property or shed has specific requirements, our team can help you understand what to confirm.

01 How should I prepare my site before delivery?

Check your available space, foundation requirements, drainage and delivery access. Follow the specifications for your selected shed model and confirm any preparation requirements with your supplier.

02 How much does shed delivery cost?

Delivery charges can depend on your location, shed size, access conditions and the supplier's delivery terms. Request a quote that clearly explains the applicable charges.

03 Is professional installation included?

Installation availability and pricing depend on the selected product and service package. Confirm whether assembly, anchoring, foundation preparation and finishing are included in your quote.

04 What if my property has narrow access?

Measure gates, pathways and tight turns, and mention any slopes or overhead obstructions before booking. The supplier can advise whether the planned delivery method is suitable.

05 Can I choose my preferred delivery date?

You can ask about your preferred date, but availability depends on the supplier's schedule and delivery area. Wait for confirmation before making other arrangements.

06 Do I need permission to install a shed?

Requirements vary by location, shed dimensions, intended use and property rules. Check the applicable local regulations and any property or community restrictions before installation.
