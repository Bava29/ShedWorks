Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help you plan your next building.

What types of buildings does ShedWorks offer?
Explore storage sheds, workshop spaces, garage-style buildings and other outdoor building options. Available styles depend on the products you offer.
Can I customize my building?
Customization may include building size, exterior finish, roof style, doors and windows. Contact us to confirm the available options for your chosen building.
How do I choose the right size?
Start by listing what you plan to store or use inside. Then consider the available property space, access, and room needed to move around comfortably.
Do you provide delivery and installation?
Delivery and installation availability can vary by building and location. Contact our team to confirm the service options for your property.
How can I get a quote?
Share the type of building you need, approximate size, preferred features and location through our contact page to discuss the next steps.
