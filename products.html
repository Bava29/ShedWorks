NEED A LITTLE CLARITY?

Questions Before You Choose?

Find helpful information to make choosing your outdoor building easier.

Still deciding on the right building?

Every property and project is different. Review these common questions or contact our team if you need help understanding your options.

Ask Our Team
Tip: Measure your available space before comparing building dimensions.
How do I choose the right shed size?

Start by listing the items you plan to store and measuring your available space. Check the building's external dimensions, door opening and the clearance needed around it.

Which building material should I choose?

Compare the materials available in our catalogue based on appearance, maintenance requirements, weather suitability and intended use. Review each product's specifications before deciding.

Can I customize my building?

Customization depends on the selected building. Check the available size, color, door and other configuration options on the individual product details page.

Do I need a permit for my shed?

Requirements vary by location, building size, placement and intended use. Check with your local building authority before purchasing or installing a shed.

How should I prepare the installation area?

Preparation depends on the building and its installation instructions. Confirm the required foundation, ground conditions, drainage and access before arranging installation.

How can I check delivery and installation options?

Delivery coverage, costs and installation availability depend on the product and service area. Contact our team to confirm the options available for your location.
